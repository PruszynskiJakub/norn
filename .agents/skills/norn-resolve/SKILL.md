# Resolve

A person is here because something needed them.

1. Find the reason the issue was flagged for a person. Read what led up to it (the review or QA comments, the PR, the spec) before bringing it to the user.
2. Lay out the cause and the options for the user, with your recommendation. When the way forward is a decision with branches of its own, invoke the `norn-interview` skill and settle it with them.
3. Carry out what was decided. A decision that changes behaviour or scope goes into the spec too.

Done when the issue can continue where it stopped. The result is the outcome, recorded on the issue.
